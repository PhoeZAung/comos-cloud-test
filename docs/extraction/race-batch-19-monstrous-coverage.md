## Lizardman

Source: https://finalfantasyd20.com/races/monstrous-races/lizardman/

## Syricta

Source: https://finalfantasyd20.com/races/monstrous-races/syricta/

- REVIEW: Elemental Resistance (six element choices) and Breath Weapon shape (cone/line) are both mandatory; current wizard lacks race-specific choice storage, so default Syricta build is save-blocked.
- REVIEW: Fly 40 ft (poor) and land 20 ft are separate; flight needs space for wing extension, land speed unaffected by armor/encumbrance.
- REVIEW: Dragon Type modifies creature type and three standard traits; not safe to apply until verified.
- REVIEW: Evasive Maneuvers describes base flight as 'clumsy' but the standard speed trait says 'poor'; source conflict requires review.
- REVIEW: Memories of the Forgotten replaces ONLY the fly speed portion of Base Speed, and requires picking two Knowledge skills; not implemented.
- REVIEW: Neglected Heritage removes only Draconic, not Common or all bonus language options; partial language replacement unresolved.
- REVIEW: Wing-Clipped changes fly speed/maneuverability and cancels -2 DEX racial penalty, not the whole racial ability vector; unresolved.
- REVIEW: Breath Weapon damage progression and 3/day uses require manual tracking.
- REVIEW: Monstrous race category exceeds 15 RP and may require GM approval.
