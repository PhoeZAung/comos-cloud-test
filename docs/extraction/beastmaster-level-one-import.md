# Beastmaster first-level integration

New first-level Beastmasters can choose one of the eleven ordinary animals listed in the companion source, or explicitly begin without a companion. The saved choice produces a companion reference card and survives creation, editing and reopening. The builder keeps that starting selection when editing; the source's 24-hour replacement ceremony remains manual. Later starters, magical beasts, plants, vermin, archetypes, multiclassing, manual-sheet adoption and levels above 1 remain guarded.

The adapter applies d10 starting HP, BAB +1, Fort/Ref/Will +2/+2/+0, four skill ranks plus Intelligence modifier (minimum one), average 105 gil, printed proficiencies and Nature Sense's +2 Knowledge (nature)/Survival. It grants the two specific Limit Break cards without adding Mounted Combat, Improved Unarmed Strike or companion feats to the character. Racial choices and existing skill/override behavior continue through the shared compiler.

Animal Focus provides a manually reset one-minute counter. Aspect effects, companion/dead-companion focus and stacking remain manual, without permanent stat or skill changes. Shared Senses is a conditional first-level feature from the companion rules and appears only when a starting companion is selected. Its daily-use counter follows Wisdom modifier, with zero available uses for a nonpositive modifier. These counters preserve expenditure across saves and ability edits and are not automatically refilled by the generic rest button. Both Limit Breaks use the existing shared Limit Break pool.

Companion HP, skill/feat/trick allocation, training, attacks, special abilities and ongoing conditions are explicitly manual. The reference card includes the selected species' starting statistics and abilities, the FFd20 2d10/full-BAB first-level companion baseline and Link. It does not grant later advancement or mastery powers or change its owner's HP, natural armor or feats. Missing species natural armor remains unspecified. Choosing no companion does not count as a dead companion or grant its extra focus.

All 35 species remain archived in the canonical family definition with their group prerequisites. Only the eleven ordinary first-level records, without later advancement sections, reach the runtime projection. Source conflicts and independent approval remain recorded; this first-level milestone does not complete the 20-level class or its archetypes.
