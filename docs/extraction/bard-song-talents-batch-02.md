# Bard extraction — Song Talents (batch 02)
Official source: https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/bard/song-talents/

## Coverage
- Base Bard class remains a staged rules record: 20 progression rows, 35 base features, 64 upgrades.
- This batch adds 32 normal Song Talents and 12 advanced Song Talents. All 44 are linked to the source table. No archetype has been activated.
- Song Talents are chosen at even Bard levels 2–20; from level 12 onward an advanced talent can replace that level's normal talent choice, not add a choice.
- Three normal talents are expressly repeatable: Attract Minion, Bard Training, Coordinate (max +5 after five selections).
- Asterisk-marked talents are different song modifiers; at most one modifier is applied to an individual song when performed, but learning multiple different asterisk talents is permitted.
- Bodyguard I/II/III and Trust use chained prerequisite IDs; class feature prerequisites refer to the actual Bard class feature IDs.
- Bardic Inspiration's die becomes d8 at 5, d10 at 10 and d12 at 15. These are state replacements on ONE option ID, not duplicate talent grants.

## Remain blocked from Archer-style live save
Bard Songs and Jingles song catalogs, Versatile Performance options, external feat selection, minion sheets, resource controls, CHA songcasting, spell/song prerequisites and Bard-specific save/level-up adapter are not yet implemented.
This catalog is extracted and validated, not marked approved, playable or live. Its effects are manual descriptions, not global always-on buffs. The current wizard will display Bard as disabled until an adapter is tested.
