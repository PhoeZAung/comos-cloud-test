# Race replacement rules: source-first, minimal wizard

**Source:** 63 existing Comos race definitions (canonical Dwarf/Elvaan/Galka and staged race definitions). This is a runtime policy, not re-extraction.

1. With resolved whole-feature source operations, replace the target traits atomically. Apply source-defined automatic bonuses only when supported by the existing sheet adapter; keep situational modifiers in trait descriptions.
2. Unsupported **descriptive** choice pools do not prevent an otherwise resolved alternate from being selected or saved. Show their source option names as guidance in the trait details. The player may record the situational choice manually on their sheet; no unnecessary popup is added. For example, Dwarf **Mountain Familarity** replaces Bond to the Land and leaves its selected terrain and +2 situational dodge AC in the description, not a permanent AC field.
3. Choice pools driving **automatic ability-score changes** still require a verified structured picker. Hume Dual Talent and Alchemically Enhanced are examples. Missing or unknown pool definitions remain blocked.
4. Source-unresolved or part-of-feature replacements remain blocked (Dwarf Saltbeard; Beastkin Alluring). Existing class/race compatibility guards remain.
5. Existing replacement selections, including heritage, are locked once a character is saved. Sheet notes and manually applied conditional bonuses remain editable. Preventing changes applies equally to level-up and edits.
6. Review the actual mechanics of each trait separately; source resolution proves replacement ownership, not complete automation of every effect.

The policy operates generically on all 63 race definitions, not just Dwarf. It deliberately leaves nonreplacement class and archetype systems untouched.
