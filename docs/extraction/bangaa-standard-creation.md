# Bangaa standard-trait creation

Source: https://finalfantasyd20.com/races/base-races/bangaa/
Archived HTML SHA-256: `1386114bb75a56b9f597011b4822a6773081b03b00cd803404380052917b27bd`.

Standard Bangaa use the printed +2 Dexterity, +2 Wisdom, −2 Intelligence vector, rather than inferring Strength/Constitution bonuses from flavor text. Identity is Medium humanoid with the reptilian subtype, land speed 30, Common/Banganese and low-light vision. Perception and Acrobatics receive +2. Optional bonus languages use six source choices (Draconic, Giant, Ignan, Numish, Seeq, Terran), bounded by the positive Intelligence modifier after the racial penalty.

Thick-skinned contributes one owned point of natural armor. Recompilation preserves manual adjustments and does not accumulate it; disabling the trait removes only its contribution. Natural armor affects normal/flat-footed AC, not touch AC. Stability remains conditional on being grounded and resisting bull rush/trip, with no global CMD bonus.

Desert Delver's environmental checks, temperature treatment and Endurance qualification, fire/ice resistance 5, bite primary/secondary attack handling and Deft Pugilist armed/no-AoO rules remain manual source cards. No bite weapon, general Endurance feat or Improved Unarmed Strike feat is inferred. Variant heritages/alternates are guarded; favored rewards and independent full review remain pending. Each class retains its existing progression boundary.
