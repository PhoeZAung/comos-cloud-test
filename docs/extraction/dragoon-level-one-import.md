# Dragoon first-level integration

Source: https://finalfantasyd20.com/classes/core-classes/dragoon/
Archived HTML SHA-256: `1d9f5fced026e63c130099f80e6af5f275ca76e0d8f48dcfb5a9b05a9d8993d1`, matching the import catalog.

The canonical definition transcribes all twenty progression rows, the four first-level class features and the two specific Limit Breaks. Later feature sections are retained as unmapped source prose, not grant/upgrade events. First-level baseline is d10 HP, BAB +1, Fort/Ref/Will +2/+2/+0, four skill ranks plus Intelligence, average 175 gil, simple weapons plus polearm/spear weapon groups and all armor, without shields.

Deadly Lancer, Jump, Pole Fighting and Strengthened Agility are manual reference cards. Jump-only Acrobatics/Strength bonuses do not become general Acrobatics modifiers; the Run treatment applies to the jump check and does not grant a general Run feat. Deadly Lancer's qualifying attacks, altitude bonus and class-level dive caps remain manual, and its extra damage is not multiplied on critical hits. The Limit Breaks share the existing usage pool, while their temporary jump/armor/HP effects remain manual.

Later progression, talents, Super Jump, dragon blood, archetypes and multiclassing are outside the bounded first-level scope. The source's narrative does not grant first-level flight or invulnerability. Structural staging is not independent approval.
