BARD MAINTAINED-SONG INTERACTION RULES
-------------------------------------
1. Maintained songs need one free action each round. If the Bard cannot take that free action (including paralysis or unconsciousness), the maintained effects end.
2. One maintained song at a time unless the Bard's Medley feature increases the limit.
3. Normal songs can be cast while a maintained song runs. A normal song requiring concentration can coexist, but blocks beginning another song until concentration ends.
4. A creature departing the maintained song's range retains the effect until the beginning of its next turn.
5. At the start of the Bard's turn, the Bard may change the targets of a maintained song that has a target-count limit, immediately ending its effect on previous targets.
6. For maintained songs requiring a save, the target saves every round until it fails; thereafter the ongoing effect remains until its duration ends or it is dismissed.
7. Against a target with spell resistance, the Bard retries a caster level check each round until successful; later rounds of the same song do not repeat that check.
8. Beginning a song provokes attacks of opportunity and uses concentration-interruption rules like spells.

RULE DIFFERENCES / ISSUES TO REVIEW
-----------------------------------
[ ] Song of Serenity: current live site SUPPRESSES fatigue while maintained, but older PDF says instant CURE. Keep original fatigue state and check at table.
[ ] Ghost Sound uses caster-level sound volume and range, not a flat 30 ft.
[ ] Lullaby is an area spell and must not become a global -5 Perception passive effect.
[ ] Jingles do not spend MP or require Perform checks; casting itself still has action and interruption rules.
[ ] Maintained saves/spell resistance have repeat-until-first-success/first-failure behavior; do not apply blanket modifiers.
[ ] No effects should apply to the saved character automatically until a vetted Bard adapter exists.
