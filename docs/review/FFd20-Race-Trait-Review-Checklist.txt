WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
-------------------
You do not need to read JSON or re-approve every normal racial trait.

A = RULINGS / UNSAFE REPLACEMENTS. These are specific alternates or heritages
    where source wording or exact replacement is not safe to auto-apply.
    Tell me "Accept" to keep the suggested safe behavior until implementation,
    or tell me exactly what should replace what.

C = WIZARD CHOICE CONTROLS. These need a working choice selector, not usually
    a debate about the rule. We should build the control and persist the choice.

R = SOURCE AND IMPLEMENTATION NOTES. These flag other exact rules, conditions,
    wording contradictions, scaling or automation. Many require coding, not
    your judgment. Reply "No change" if the suggested interpretation is fine.
